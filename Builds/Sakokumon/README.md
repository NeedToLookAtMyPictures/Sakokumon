# Sakokumon

Sakokumon is a game designed to show the evolution of trade and cultural exchange between Japan and the Western world during the Sakoku period.

By taking the place of a guard on the only bridge into and out of Dejima island, the player oversees people coming and going to the island, the only point of trade with the Dutch (and by extension, Europe in general).

The player must keep track of varying rules and regulations as they inspect the goods that travelers bring with them, flagging anybody attempting to smuggle illegal goods.

By seeing the varied people moving into and out of the isolated island, and learning about the various regulations on goods put into place and/or removed over time, players can learn more about the culture of Japan during the Sakoku period, with a focus on the exchange with the West.
